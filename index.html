Current collection slots are Saturday 10:00–12:00 and Sunday 10:00–13:00.

Where is collection?

Self-collection is in Singapore. The available collection details are shown during checkout.

How do I order?

Choose your drinks, add them to your order, select a collection slot, and complete checkout.
